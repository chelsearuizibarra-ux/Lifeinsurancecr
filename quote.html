Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mortgage Protection Insurance? +
Mortgage protection insurance is an affordable term life insurance policy designed to cover your mortgage if you pass away or experience a qualifying health event, ensuring your family can stay in their home.
Who receives payouts from my policy? +
If you pass away, the benefit goes to your named beneficiary to pay off the mortgage. If you trigger a living benefit — like a critical illness diagnosis — you receive the payout directly while you're still alive.
Are mortgage protection quotes free? +
Yes — completely free with no purchase obligation. A licensed agent will walk you through your options with zero pressure.
Can I apply if I have a health condition? +
Most likely, yes. Many carriers offer policies for people with pre-existing conditions. A licensed agent will find the best available plan for your situation.
